# Release Notes - v1.0.0

## Attanagalla Pradeshiya Sabha - Waste Management, Recycling & Compost Intelligence Platform
**Initial Official Release (v1.0.0)**  
**Date:** October 23, 2026  
**Author:** Muhammad Zaharan (@muhzahjr07)

---

### 🌟 Overview
This release provides an end-to-end OCR digitized audit, bilingual analytical reporting suite, and executive dashboard for the solid waste management, recyclable material sales, and organic fertilizer production operations of the **Attanagalla Pradeshiya Sabha** (Gampaha District, Western Province, Sri Lanka) from 2022 to 2026.

---

### 🚀 Key Highlights & Deliverables
1. **Bilingual Executive Dashboard (`Attanagalla_Waste_Management_Report.html`):**
   - Interactive language switcher: **සිංහල (Sinhala)**, **English**, and **Dual Bilingual Mode**.
   - Chart.js visualizations for Revenue Share, Multi-Year Inflow Growth, Monthly Collections, and Price Realization.
   - Built-in PDF/Print optimization styling for administrative and council meetings.

2. **Full Research & Analytical Audit Document (`Attanagalla_Waste_Management_Report.md`):**
   - Official 2026 Recyclables Collection & Sales Summary (9 material categories, LKR 2.187M Jan–Jul revenue, LKR 3.15M+ cumulative).
   - Multi-year solid waste intake audit (2022–2026) with detailed YoY percentage increases and 2022 baseline benchmarking (+196% cumulative growth).
   - Technical and empirical engineering analysis of **Tractor Trailer Solid Waste Carrying Capacity** (0.8 – 1.2 MT average, peaking at 1.5 – 2.0 MT wet).
   - Gannoruwa Central Soil & Fertilizer Testing Laboratory chemical analysis of organic compost vs. SLS 1635:2019 / SLS 645 standards.

3. **High-Resolution Visual Assets (`assets/screenshots/`):**
   - `dashboard_overview.png`: High-resolution overview of the executive dashboard and metric cards.
   - `full_dashboard.png`: Complete page capture of all tables, analysis cards, and strategic action plans.

4. **Data Repository (`data/`):**
   - 28 high-resolution primary source images of official printed statements, handwritten daily intake logs (days 1–31), commercial ledgers, and testing certificates.
